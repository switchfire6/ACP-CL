## Findings and interpretation

Both primary replication screens fail. In raw Brier units, conditional
T is -.0004391 [-.0013995, +.0004062], positive in 6/12 seeds; recurrent T is
+.0008252 [-.0000484, +.0017904], positive in 8/12. The latter passes the
magnitude requirement but misses the fixed 9/12 consistency requirement.
These are twelve independent seed averages per architecture, each containing
two assessments. Neither architecture establishes the declared replication of
lost selection usefulness. This weakens that specific explanation of the prior
cohort; it does not establish state-independent memory value or equivalence.

The relative-versus-absolute warning in the pre-outcome review matters here.
Conditional selected error increases from .0733158 at 000 to .0739026 at 111,
while uniform candidate error increases more, from .0739663 to .0749923.
The improved selection contrast therefore is not evidence of improved absolute
learning. Those absolute changes are themselves uncertain. A predeclared
recurrent weight reversion also improves mean D slightly while increasing
selected absolute Brier by .002457. Reverting a component to improve its
ranking contrast is not a justified learning rule.

All current-branch averaged main-effect intervals and all six current-branch
selection-repair intervals include zero. Several conditional effects and
interactions differ, and the mixed weight/Adam states are artificial. The
factorial supports a description of the defined substitutions, not a unique
natural cause or a winning component to restore. No subgroup, assessment age,
favorable interval, or architecture can substitute for the failed screens.

At 111, selected extra rehearsal versus the matching zero-update reference
changes current Brier by -.0000081 [-.0019202, +.0019820] conditional and
+.0004658 [-.0006073, +.0016236] recurrent. Neither establishes an absolute
benefit from these extra twelve updates. These zero controls omit only the
diagnostic extra rehearsal: their parent learners already received ordinary
uniform replay. They do not show that replay itself is unnecessary.

Current-to-return rank agreement with cell 111 fixed is +.0397 conditional and
-.0218 recurrent. Returning-branch D111 is +.0009053 [-.0010944, +.0029400]
and +.0003923 [-.0012709, +.0022677], respectively. Ranking transfer remains
weak in these finite windows. The branch changes physical consequences and
subsequent causal feedback together; it does not isolate a pure environment
effect with context fixed. Only 10/24 assessments contain an exact return-origin
candidate, and the predictive selector chooses one in 0/24. All assessments
properly remain included; origin is neither an explanation sufficient to
excuse failure nor a guarantee of usefulness.

The return forecasts hold learned parameters fixed and never train the ordinary
trajectory on returns. Their substantial errors cannot establish inability to
relearn, feature erasure, or the benefit of a particular learning repair. The
study does not measure cumulative improvement under an adaptive memory policy,
longer physical planning, or sustained acquisition of new representations.
The current report's principal numerical and interpretive statements agree
with these completed outputs and the pre-outcome decision branches.

## Bounded next direction

Stop extending the cached-score and component-rollback diagnostic line for now.
Retain ordinary uniform replay as the working reference. The next question
should change the learner's actual continuing training path and address the
charter's unresolved requirement: acquiring a genuinely new predictive
dependency while retaining knowledge that remains useful.

A trained reusable core plus a small adaptive residual, compared with the
same-capacity network trained jointly, is a reasonable single architectural
hypothesis to specify next. Both must start from random initialization across
their complete learning lifetime, share the prefix and uniform replay,
receive identical observations and update exposure, and have explicit total
parameter, trainable-parameter, memory and compute accounting. A residual
that sees only an information-losing frozen embedding cannot recover omitted
cues; its access to raw observations or independently trainable features must
be declared. Introduce a previously unneeded dependency late, include valid-old
and return evaluations, and judge acquisition together with retention. A known
experimental transition must not silently become an oracle available to the
proposed deployed mechanism.

This would test whether separating stable computation from adaptive capacity
improves the measured tradeoff. A frozen-core success alone would not establish
continued acquisition of new core representations or autonomous lifetime
operation. Any later consolidation or capacity renewal would need its own test.
Do not derive numerical gates or choose a residual width from this cohort.

I would not place a separate extra-rehearsal necessity study before that
architectural test if rehearsal work stays unchanged. It would address a
different dose intervention, and earlier ordinary-replay comparisons already
provide a reason to retain that baseline. If a future mechanism adds rehearsal
work, then an actual continued-trajectory comparison against no extra work
and a matched-work control becomes necessary. The present frozen short forks
cannot establish its cumulative benefit or cost.
