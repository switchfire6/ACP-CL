# Acquisition diagnostic development ledger

The accepted next step is diagnostic: test genuinely new predictive dependencies,
valid retention, and obsolete relationships around the existing two learners.
No novel update mechanism is being evaluated or tuned in this study.

The first structural world check exposed a metric problem before any learner
training: changing efficiency or delay affects survival probabilities, but the
best one-time transfer need not change. Their matched-pair clairvoyant action
value was zero when introduced alone. Supply timing did change optimal actions.
Record this openly and use predictive Brier error as the acquisition endpoint;
keep action survival as a separate outcome. No learner outcome selected this
endpoint or the fixed physical rules. Before activation every cue is irrelevant,
and clean/immediate-channel outcomes remain identical throughout the additions.

The prospective protocol declares fresh-only development on seeds 211/212,
the exposure grid 2048/4096/8192, and all qualification thresholds. Freeze the
physical rules and learner architectures for that grid. Preserve every attempt.
The six evaluation seeds 7001--7006 and continuing/frozen/reset-state comparisons
must not select settings. If fresh learners fail qualification, stop scaling
and report the inadequate diagnostic instead of interpreting architecture effects.

Before learner development, clarify that the two qualification thresholds must
pass for each stage AND each dependency type within each architecture. This
prevents averaging a weakly learned cue together with an easier one.

## Development selection

The first budget, 2,048 arrivals per episode, passed every declared marginal-
prediction and cue-use check for both architectures, grouped by stage and by
new dependency. All 12 fresh-only fits completed. No larger budget was tried;
configs for 4,096 and 8,192 remain unused declared grid entries, not failed runs.
Select 2,048 arrivals for every new-dependency arm and final branch. The smallest
cue-use margin was .002499 for recurrent arrival-delay prediction, above .002.
The fresh cohort must independently recheck adequacy; the narrow margin is not
a reason to adjust its threshold after observing new outcomes.

All 12 development before/final checkpoint pairs passed local fork, memory,
training-data and probe recomputation audits. The 30-episode engineering smoke
also passed. Scientific source has not changed since fresh-only development.
